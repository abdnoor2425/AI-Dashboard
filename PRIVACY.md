# Privacy policy

AI-Dashboard is an open-source dashboard that each person downloads and runs on their own computer.

**What it reads.** When you connect a Google app, the dashboard asks Google for read-only access to that app only:
- Google Analytics: your website list and visitor numbers.
- Gmail: senders and subject lines of unread mail. It never reads the body of an email. It can send an email only if you turn on email alerts yourself.
- Google Drive: the names and dates of recent files. It never opens their contents.
- Google Calendar: your events for the next seven days.

**Where it goes.** Everything stays on your own computer, in the dashboard's `.keystone` folder. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server and keep no copy of your data.

**Removing access.** Click Disconnect on the Connections page, or remove AI-Dashboard at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the `.keystone` folder deletes everything the dashboard stored.

AI-Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/abdnoor2425/AI-Dashboard/issues.
